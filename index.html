READY TO WARM UP?

Make every click count.

Open the ODRIVEX aim trainer to practice in your browser. This is a standalone practice tool and does not change game files or in-game recoil.

QUICK START

Build a smoother setup.

Try these reversible steps first, especially on an integrated-graphics PC.

STEP 01

Reduce background load

Close apps you do not need and review Startup Apps in Windows Settings.

STEP 02

Tune the emulator

If using MSI App Player, test a lower resolution and a stable FPS cap when frames stutter.

STEP 03

Practice consistently

Keep mouse DPI and sensitivity consistent while you train. Change one setting at a time.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Does ODRIVEX remove Free Fire recoil?

No. The aim trainer helps you practise mouse accuracy, but it does not modify in-game aim, recoil, or game files.

Will it guarantee more FPS?

No tool can guarantee an FPS increase. Results depend on your PC, emulator settings, drivers, and what is running in the background.

Does this website need a login?

No. This demo website does not collect login details or require an account.

Can I customize the website later?

Yes. You can edit the title, colors, links, features, and contact details in the HTML file.
